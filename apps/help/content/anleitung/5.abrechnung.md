One price for the whole community, not per product. 14 days free, cancellable
at any time.

## The plans

| | **No subscription** | **Personal** | **Pro** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price per month | no subscription | €29 | €149 | individual |
| Paid yearly | — | €21.75 / month | €111.75 / month | individual |
| Who it's for | Reading only | Coaches and creators | Teams | Your own instance |

All amounts are **final prices including 19 % VAT** — monthly and yearly alike.

Paying yearly gets you **three months free** — €261 instead of €348 a year for
Personal, €1,341 instead of €1,788 for Pro (also including 19 % VAT). That is
the same 25 % as before, just put more tangibly.

**Basic** is not an offering but the state of being *without a subscription*:
the community stays readable and fully intact, but nothing is written in it.

**Enterprise** is not a self-service plan but the *Pukalani Studio* offering:
your own instance, your own domain, with consulting, concept, design and
technical implementation. Priced by effort — talk to us.

## Quotas

So that a single community doesn't take up the shared server, there are upper
limits on comments:

| Plan | per day | in total |
|---|---|---|
| No subscription | 200 | 5,000 |
| Personal | 1,000 | 50,000 |
| Pro | 5,000 | 250,000 |

"Per day" means a **rolling 24-hour window**, not a calendar day. No such limit
applies to Enterprise instances — they run on infrastructure of their own.

## Trial period

- **14 days of Pro**, no payment details, no cancellation needed.
- The trial starts at the moment your community is created.
- After that the community is **read-only** until a plan is chosen: posts,
  comments and sign-ups pause. Content, members and settings are kept in full —
  nothing is deleted, and with a subscription (Personal or Pro) everything
  continues immediately.

## How many communities

- **One** during the trial period.
- **Up to three** afterwards, per account.

This staggering is an abuse brake: a fresh account can occupy exactly one
address.

## Changing plan

The plan belongs to your **community**. As the owner you find it in the
dashboard under **Community settings → Plan** (bottom left in the menu): there
you choose the target plan and the billing period (monthly or yearly) and end
up in the **Stripe** checkout — after which you come back to exactly that tab.

Payment methods, invoices and cancellation are managed in the Stripe customer
portal, which you open from the same tab.

::note
There is **no minimum term**. If you cancel, the paid period runs out — and you
take your content with you via export.
::

## Who may handle billing

Only the **owner** of the community — the other roles (admin, moderator,
editor) don't even see the Plan tab. Who that is and how ownership is handed
over: [Members & roles](/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen).
