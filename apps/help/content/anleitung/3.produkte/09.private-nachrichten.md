::note
**From the Personal plan — and off by default.** Direct messages are the only
place in Pukalani where two people write without the community reading along.
That is why the owner deliberately decides whether they exist.
::

A private conversation between **two members of the same community**: one
conversation, a history inside it, read and unread, following along live. That
is all it is — and for now that is all it is meant to be.

## Switching it on

The **owner** switches it on under **Dashboard → Community settings → Direct
messages**. Before that it doesn't exist: existing communities stay as they
are, nothing is opened up quietly.

If the switch is off, the *Messages* menu entry is still there, but the page
says plainly: "This community has not switched on direct messages."

## Writing

The way in today is the **inbox** under **Dashboard → Messages**, then **New
message**. You address people by their **short name** (without the `@`) — not
by an email address and not from a list of all members. Anyone who doesn't know
a short name cannot write to anybody; that is deliberate, not a shortcoming.

::note
A "write a message" button next to a person's name does **not exist yet**. It
is planned, but not wired up.
::

## Who may write to whom

| Action | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Opening** a conversation | [Trust level 1](/anleitung/produkte/diskussionen) — or an appointed role (editor, moderator, admin, owner) |
| **Replying** when someone has written to you | no level needed |
| **Receiving** | every member |

So a plain **viewer without trust level 1 cannot start** — that is exactly
where the spam protection bites. Replying is always allowed.

## Limits that always apply

So that a messaging route doesn't become an advertising channel, there are
three brakes — per community and person:

| Brake | Value |
|---|---|
| New conversations per day | 10 from level 1 · 30 from level 2 |
| Messages per minute | 20 |
| Open, unanswered conversations | at most 5 |

The last one is the most effective: someone who has written to five people and
received no answer writes to no sixth.

## Blocking and reporting

- **Blocking** works in **both directions** and without announcement — the
  blocked person is not told. If you want, the block also applies in your other
  communities. Blocking works even if the owner switches the product off again.
- **Reporting** is possible for every individual message. The report goes into
  a list of its own at [Moderation](/anleitung/produkte/moderation).

::warning
**Moderation does not read along.** It sees **only the reported message**, and
as a frozen copy from the moment of the report — not the conversation before
it, and not the one after. A conversation is not hidden automatically either; a
human decides that.
::

If someone is turned away — because they are blocked, lack the level, or the
name doesn't exist — they always read the same sentence: "This person is not
currently accepting messages from you." The reason is deliberately left open;
otherwise the error message would be an information service about who has
blocked whom.

## Notifications

A new message appears in the bell; several messages in the same conversation
are summarised. If you have switched on emails, you get a mail with the
**sender's name and a link — without the text of the message**.

## Tidying up

**Remove for me** hides a conversation on your side. It is only deleted for
good once **both** sides have removed it. If the other person writes again
afterwards, the history is back.

Your messages are part of the data export and are deleted along with your
account.

## What doesn't exist today

- **No group messages** — one-to-one only.
- **No search in the inbox.**
- **No attachments** and no images.
- **No read receipt** that the sender can see.
- **No replying by email** — the mail only links.
- **No personal "receive no messages"**: anyone who wants to be left alone
  blocks specific people; only the owner can switch it off, and then for
  everyone.
