Where comments hang *under* something, posts are the *something*: your members
write for themselves, and the feed shows what is happening, in order.

## Three kinds of post

| Kind | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Post** | The normal case: title (optional) and text in Markdown |
| **Poll** | Up to six options, optionally with a closing date |
| **Question** | Like a post, but marked as a question |

The text is Markdown (up to 10,000 characters), titles up to 200. Raw HTML is
allowed nowhere — that holds for all content in Pukalani.

## Polls

- Up to **six options** of 100 characters each.
- Every member has **one vote**.
- The **results become visible** once you have voted yourself or the poll has
  closed — until then nobody sees the running count. That stops the first vote
  from pulling the rest along.
- With a closing date the poll ends by itself.

## Votes

Posts can be voted up and down. The counting is done by the server, not the
browser — so the number on screen cannot be manipulated.

## The states of a post

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scheduled** | Time set; until then visible only to the author |
| **Published** | Visible in the feed |
| **Hidden** | Removed by moderation (can be restored) |
| **Deleted** | Withdrawn by the author |

## Comments and moderation

[Comments](/anleitung/produkte/kommentare) run under every post. Posts can be
reported, hidden and restored — the tools for that are described in
[Moderation](/anleitung/produkte/moderation).

Any signed-in member can write; hiding and restoring is for the **moderator**
role and above (see
[Members & roles](/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen)).
