Your community rarely speaks just one language. That's why every piece of
content carries a **Translate** button: one click, and the text appears in
the language the reader is currently using. **Show original** brings back
the original wording at any time.

## What gets translated

- **Posts and questions** — title and body.
- **Comments** — including the [embedded widget](/entwickler/embed-widget)
  on your own website.
- **Polls** — question, body **and every answer option**. Votes are never
  affected: what counts is the position, not the wording.
- **Events** — title and description.
- **Courses** — the course description and every lesson.

Markdown formatting, `code`, links and @-mentions are left untouched. Mixed-
language texts work too: whatever is already in the target language stays,
the rest gets translated.

## One translates, everyone benefits

The first person to translate a piece of content into their language triggers
the translation — after that it is **stored**. Every further reader of the
same language sees it instantly, with no waiting. If the original gets
edited, the outdated translation disappears automatically; the next click
creates a fresh one.

## Who can translate

The button is visible to **signed-in members**. Guests read the original —
this keeps the feature safe from automated abuse. A generous daily allowance
per account ensures fair use; stored translations never count against it.

## Translating never shows more than reading

Translation respects every access boundary: a lesson that requires an
enrollment or a subscription only hands out its text as a translation once
you have that access. Hidden or deleted content cannot be translated.

::note
**Custom pages** (imprint, terms, your own content pages) are deliberately
not auto-translated: they are your editorial texts — what you publish is
what counts. Multilingual pages are maintained per language by you.
::
