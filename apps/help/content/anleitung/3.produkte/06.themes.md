Nothing gives away an off-the-shelf platform faster than its off-the-shelf
look. So you decide your community's colour and typeface yourself — in the
dashboard under **Themes**.

## The catalogue: 26 colour worlds

The built-in catalogue holds **26 colour worlds** — from Crimson and Coral
through Lagoon and Denim to Graphite and Espresso. Each world comes with
**eleven shades**: the base tone plus ten tonal variants, always under the same
names.

| Variant | Effect |
|---|---|
| `soft`, `muted`, `pastel` | held back, less colour |
| `vivid`, `bright`, `deep` | richer, lighter or deeper |
| `warm`, `cool` | shifted towards warmer or cooler tones |
| `dawn`, `dusk` | shifted noticeably — morning and evening mood |

Because the names are the same everywhere, you can compare worlds instead of
clicking your way through arbitrary colours. You choose on a screen of preview
tiles: first the world, then the shade.

## Custom themes

If the catalogue isn't enough, you create a theme of your own — you give **one
base colour** and the platform works out the rest. A contrast check steps in
automatically and shifts colour steps far enough that text stays readable. So
you cannot pick a colour that makes your community unreadable.

You can also set, among other things, the neutral base tone (neutral or
slightly tinted), the corner rounding and the treatment of headings (weight,
letter spacing, capitalisation).

## Light and dark

Both modes come out of the same definition — there is nothing to maintain
twice. Visitors follow their system setting or switch for themselves.

## Fonts

There are exactly **two freely choosable roles**: one typeface for body text
and one for headings (plus a fixed monospace for code). More than three
typefaces on a page get restless — that is a deliberate limit.

- From the **registry**: curated families that the platform serves itself. No
  call to a third-party font server, so no tracking arises here either.
- **Your own fonts**: upload WOFF2 files (Dashboard → Themes → Fonts). The file
  is checked on upload and afterwards served from your own address.

::warning
With your own fonts, mind the licence — web embedding is not permitted for
every typeface you buy.
::

## Changes take effect immediately

When you switch colour world or typeface, **open windows change without
reloading** — including for visitors who are reading right now. So you can try
things live instead of speculating.

In the editor you also see a preview of your draft; if you leave it without
saving, the published state is restored.

## Who may change the design

The **admin** role and above (see
[Members & roles](/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen)).
