Pages are the fixed texts of your community: home page, "About us", your ground
rules for conversation, imprint, terms, privacy policy. You look after them in
the dashboard under **Pages**.

## How a page is put together

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Address (slug)** | The path the page is reachable at — lower-case letters, digits, hyphens, no slashes (e.g. `rules`) |
| **Title** | Heading and page title, up to 256 characters |
| **Content** | The text in the editor (Markdown), up to 200,000 characters |
| **Status** | *Draft* (visible internally only) or *Published* |
| **Order** | Sort number for listings |

## Language versions

A page is **its own version** per language — same slug, different language.
That way you can publish your imprint in German and leave the English version
sitting as a draft, without a half-finished translation going online.

If the matching language version is missing, navigation and page fall back to
the English one — and if that is missing too, to whichever was published first.
So a link never leads nowhere; as soon as your translation is published, it
takes over automatically.

## Draft and publishing

A draft is invisible to visitors. Only *Published* makes the page public. There
is no second approval step: what you publish is live immediately.

::warning
**Legal texts are your responsibility.** When your community is created, an
imprint and a privacy policy are generated as **draft templates** with clearly
marked gaps (`[FILL IN: …]`) — they give you the structure, not your details.
Replace the markers, check the result (with a lawyer if in doubt) and only then
publish; a draft is never publicly visible. There is deliberately no template
for terms and conditions.
::

## Where published pages appear

Published pages appear automatically in your community's main navigation,
sorted by their order number. Two exceptions: `home` is the home page itself
(the logo links to it), and **legal pages** — the addresses `imprint`,
`impressum`, `privacy`, `datenschutz`, `terms` and `agb` — sit in the footer
instead of the main navigation.

## Who may edit pages

The **editor** role and above (see
[Members & roles](/en/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen)). Viewers and moderators
can read pages but not change them.
