Pukalani is made of products you can use individually. You don't have to use a
single one you don't need.

## What there is

| Product | What it does | Available |
|---|---|---|
| [Comments](/en/anleitung/produkte/kommentare) | Nested replies with votes, mentions and live presence — under any content | even without a subscription |
| [Moderation](/en/anleitung/produkte/moderation) | Report, hide, restore; optional AI second opinion | even without a subscription |
| [Pages](/en/anleitung/produkte/seiten) | Your own content pages such as home page, rules, imprint | even without a subscription |
| [Design & themes](/en/anleitung/produkte/themes) | 26 colour worlds, custom themes and fonts | even without a subscription |
| [Posts & feed](/en/anleitung/produkte/beitraege-und-feed) | Posts by your members, polls, questions | from Personal |
| [Discussions](/en/anleitung/produkte/diskussionen) | The same posts arranged by category — with states, filters, badges and trust levels | from Personal |
| [Analytics](/en/anleitung/produkte/analytics) | Visitors, page views, top pages and sources — measured without cookies | from Personal |
| [Direct messages](/en/anleitung/produkte/private-nachrichten) | One-to-one conversations between members — off by default | from Personal |
| [Custom domain](/en/anleitung/produkte/eigene-domain) | Your community at your own address, with a certificate | from Pro |
| AI assistance | A second opinion during moderation | from Pro |
| Courses | Lessons with progress and paid access | from Pro · Early Access |
| Events | Dates with RSVPs, series and tickets | from Pro · Early Access |

::note
**Early Access** means: the product is built and running, but is not yet part
of the open offering. We switch it on together — you deliberately cannot buy it
yet.
::

## How products are controlled

Two levels work together:

1. **Your plan** decides which products are unlocked for your community at all
   (see the table above and [Billing](/en/anleitung/abrechnung)).
2. **Your role** decides what you may do inside an unlocked product — read,
   write, moderate, administer (see
   [Members & roles](/en/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen)).

If a product isn't part of your plan, it doesn't show up in your community in
the first place.

## Where you manage them

As the owner, the way in is your community's **dashboard**. Every active
product brings its own area there — comments, posts, pages, themes. Which areas
you see depends on your role: the navigation hides what you wouldn't be allowed
to do anyway.
