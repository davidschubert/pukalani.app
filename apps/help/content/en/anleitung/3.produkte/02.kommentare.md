Comments aren't an add-on, they're the connective tissue: they run under posts,
under pages and — if you want — even inside your existing website.

## What members can do

- **Nested replies.** Side threads hang indented off the post they answer
  instead of mixing into the main thread. Within a thread, new replies appear
  at the top — right below the form.
- **Votes.** Up and down; the sum is a comment's score.
- **Mentions.** An `@` before a name notifies the person mentioned.
- **Markdown.** Bold, italic, lists, links, code — safely rendered.
- **Editing and deleting** your own comments.
- **Reporting.** Any comment can be reported; the report goes to
  [Moderation](/en/anleitung/produkte/moderation).

## Sorting

Four orders to choose from:

| Mode | What ends up on top |
|---|---|
| **New** | The most recent comment first (default) |
| **Top** | The highest score first |
| **Trending** | Approval with time decay — fresh and good beats old and good |
| **Most discussed** | The threads with the most replies |

## Live: presence and real time

New comments appear **without reloading**. So that nobody shifts the text out
from under your cursor, they collect behind a pill reading "Show new comments"
until you click it.

On top of that, the presence display shows who is currently

- reading the thread,
- typing,
- replying to a particular comment.

That is the difference between a place that feels inhabited and an archive.

## Notifications

Replies and mentions create a notification in the bell. On top of that, every
member can switch on emails under **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Off** (default),
- **Immediately** — one email per event,
- **Daily digest** — at most one email per day, made from whatever has been
  left unread.

The language of those emails follows the recipient's own setting.

## Guests

You decide whether people who aren't signed in may read along. Writing without
an account is only possible in the embedded widget, and is a separate switch
there — see [Embed widget](/en/entwickler/embed-widget).

## Embedding on someone else's website

Comments can be embedded into any other page with a `<script>` tag — blog,
docs, static HTML. The technical instructions are under
[Embed widget](/en/entwickler/embed-widget).
