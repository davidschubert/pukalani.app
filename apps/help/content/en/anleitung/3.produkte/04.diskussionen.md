::note
**From the Personal plan.** Discussions and
[Posts & feed](/en/anleitung/produkte/beitraege-und-feed) are the same product:
if you have the feed, you have discussions too.
::

The feed shows everything in order — whoever wrote last is on top. The
**discussions** show exactly the same material arranged by **category**:
structure your team sets, and topics your members open inside it.

A topic is not a new kind of thing, it is **a post with a category**. That
isn't a detail, it's the reason everything familiar still applies: comments
underneath, voting up and down, reporting, moderation — none of it works
differently here than in the feed.

::note
Looking for the nested replies under a post? Those are called
[comments](/en/anleitung/produkte/kommentare) — until 4 August 2026 their guide
lived at this address.
::

## Categories belong to the team

Members **cannot** create categories. That is deliberate: structure is a
decision, not a by-product. You look after it in the dashboard under
**Discussions → Categories**.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Freely changeable at any time |
| **Address** | The path fragment (`/discussions/your-address`) — lower-case letters, digits, hyphens |
| **Description** | One sentence, shown in the category overview |
| **Order** | A smaller number sits higher up |
| **Visible** | Switch: visible or retired |

::warning
**The address is fixed once created.** Unlike a topic, a category page carries
no immutable id through which an old link could heal itself. While you are
creating it, the address follows the name automatically as long as you don't
touch it yourself.
::

These addresses are taken and cannot be assigned: `categories`, `new`, `all`,
`search`, `api`, `about`, `badges`.

**Retire instead of delete.** A retired category takes no new topics; what is
already there stays readable. Deleting only works while the category is empty —
if topics are in it, Pukalani refuses and suggests retiring instead.

There are no sub-categories.

## Opening a topic

There are **two ways in, but only one writing surface** — both open the same
composer and create the same post:

1. **From the discussions.** The **Open a topic** button sits above the topic
   list and on every category page. On a category page that category is already
   **pre-selected** — you can still change it.
2. **In the feed.** You write as usual and pick a **category**:

   > With a category, your post also appears under Discussions.

This holds for all three kinds (post, poll, question) and for **every
signed-in member**; no special role is needed. The category picker only appears
once categories exist at all — and the *Open a topic* button only when there is
a visible category.

::note
**A topic needs a category.** If you open the composer through *Open a topic*,
the "No category" option is dropped — without a category it wouldn't be a
topic but an ordinary feed post. Saving is therefore only possible once a
category is set.
::

Two directions, so there are no surprises:

- A post **with** a category still stays in the feed. A community has one
  place, not two.
- A post **without** a category does not appear under Discussions and has no
  address there.

## The topic list

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| **Topic** | State badges, title, and the category below it |
| **Author** | One click filters down to that person's topics |
| **Replies** | Number of comments |
| **Views** | How often the topic has been opened |
| **Activity** | When something last happened, relative ("5 hrs ago") |

On narrow screens author, replies and views are hidden — the title always
stays. 25 topics are loaded; **Load more** fetches the next batch.

::note
**How views are counted.** The same person counts again for the same topic at
most every 30 minutes — otherwise a held-down F5 key would be the whole
metric. People are told apart by account or IP address; the IP address itself
is **not stored**, only the number is.
::

### Sorting

| Order | What ends up on top |
|---|---|
| **Latest** | Most recently active — **pinned items sit on top here** |
| **Top** | The most approval, within a period you choose |
| **Categories** | Switches to the category overview (category + number of topics) |

**Top** adds a period: *All time*, *Year*, *Quarter*, *Month*, *Week*,
*Today*. "Today" means the calendar day, all others a rolling window (365, 90,
30 and 7 days). **Pinned items are not pulled forward under "Top"** — otherwise
an announcement would sit permanently above the leaderboard.

## Filters and search

Above the list sits **Filter** with a counter for how many are currently
active. If any are set, the section opens by itself.

| Filter | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Category** | A single category (omitted if you're inside one anyway) |
| **Created after / Created before** | A time window |
| **Topic** | All, open or closed |
| **Solution** | All, solved or unsolved |
| **Pinned only** | Hides everything else |
| **My topics only** | Your own (only when signed in) |
| **From one person** | Created by clicking an author in the list |

**Reset filters** clears everything at once. If the address bar holds a value
that doesn't exist, it is **ignored** rather than reported as an error — a
shared link should always show something.

### What the search searches

The **Search topics** field searches the **titles** — and only those. Neither
the body of a post nor the replies underneath are searched. That is the honest
limit today; if you're looking for a word from the middle of a reply, you won't
find it here.

In the category overview, the same field searches category names **and**
descriptions.

## The states of a topic

| State | What it means | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Pinned** | Sits on top under "Latest" | Moderator and above — or **trust level 4** |
| **Closed** | Takes no new comments | Moderator and above — or **trust level 4** |
| **Solved** | The question has been answered | As above — **or** whoever opened the topic |

The three are independent and can be combined. They are set on the topic under
**Manage topic**; they are shown as badges before the title.

::note
**Closed really means closed** — for moderators too. Existing comments stay and
remain readable, nothing new is added. Anyone who tries anyway reads: "This
post takes no new comments. What has been written stays."
::

Only published topics can be put into a state — a scheduled post doesn't have
one yet.

## The sidebar

On the left are at most **five categories**, and the heading says where they
come from:

- **Your categories** — the ones you most recently wrote or commented in.
- **Largest categories** — while you haven't joined in anywhere yet.

Below that **All categories** and, set apart, the signposts of the area: *About
the discussions*, *Badges* and — if the page exists — your *Rules*.

## About the discussions

The **About the discussions** page answers the question "where have I landed?":

1. **Your text.** It comes from an ordinary page with the address
   `discussions-about` (Dashboard → **Pages**). If it's missing, the spot stays
   empty; only people allowed to manage pages see a hint there.
2. **Four numbers:** total topics · topics in the last 7 days · posts today ·
   number of visible categories.
3. **Who to talk to:** leadership (owner and admins) and moderation, with names
   and pictures.

Deliberately absent: **no** numbers on active users, joins or votes cast, and
**no** links to profiles. Pukalani has no public profile pages, and a number
that is only roughly right is worse than none at all on an about page.

## Rules

Your ground rules live at the address `guidelines`, that is
`yourname.pukalani.app/guidelines`. They are an ordinary
[page](/en/anleitung/produkte/seiten) and are edited in the dashboard under
**Pages** — by the **editor** role and above.

Until you have created that page, Pukalani shows an **editable default text**
there — for new and existing communities alike. The first time you save, it
becomes your own page, and from then on only what you wrote applies. The text
is a **suggestion, not a legal document**: what ends up there is your
responsibility.

## Badges

Under **Badges** there is a gallery of **22** awards in four groups. It always
shows the whole catalogue — including what nobody has yet. That way you can see
what there is to earn in the first place.

**Approval means upvotes.** Downvotes count towards no badge.

### First steps

| Badge | What for |
|---|---|
| **Introduced** | Text about yourself and a profile picture added |
| **First approval** | Upvoted something for the first time |
| **First report** | Reported something for the first time |
| **Revised** | Reworked one of your own posts or replies for the first time |

### Community

| Badge | What for |
|---|---|
| **Welcome** | Something of yours received a vote |
| **Appreciated** | 20 of your posts or replies have at least one vote |
| **Thank you** | 20 with a vote — and 10 given yourself |
| **Gives back** | 100 with a vote — and 100 given yourself |
| **Devoted** | 500 with a vote — and 1,000 given yourself |
| **Recognised** | 100 with at least two votes |
| **Admired** | 300 with at least five votes |
| **Anniversary** | A year here — and something written in that year |

### Writing

| Badge | Post | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| 10 votes on one piece | **Good post** | **Good reply** |
| 25 votes | **Great post** | **Great reply** |
| 50 votes | **Outstanding post** | **Outstanding reply** |

### Trust level

Every level reached earns a badge: **Basic**, **Member**, **Regular** and
**Leader**. What's behind them is in the next section.

### How they are awarded

- **Some come more than once.** The six badges in the *Writing* group are
  awarded **per post or reply** that crosses the threshold — a second post with
  ten votes is a second earning. The **anniversary** comes round again **every
  membership year**. The gallery then shows the count as `×2`, `×3`. Everything
  else — states such as *Introduced* and the cumulative thresholds of the
  *Community* group — stays one-off.
- **You are notified.** An earned badge appears in the bell ("Badge earned:
  …"), and by email if you want.
- **The counting happens when writing and voting**, not only when you look. But
  opening the gallery stays as a safety net: anything that was left behind for
  whatever reason is caught up there.

::note
**"Revised" starts at zero for everyone.** Only revisions from the day the
counter was introduced are counted — earlier edits are not captured
retroactively. And only a real change to title or text counts; switching
category or state is not a revision.
::

Badges appear **only** in this gallery, not next to your name in the topic
list.

## Trust levels

People who stay involved get more responsibility — without anyone having to
hand out a role. There are five levels; the first three are earned, the fourth
is granted.

| Level | Name | How you get it |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **New** | Starting state |
| 1 | **Basic** | 2 days here · 1 piece of your own · 1 approval given |
| 2 | **Member** | 15 days · 5 pieces · 10 approvals given and 5 received |
| 3 | **Regular** | 60 days · 25 pieces · 50 approvals given and 25 received |
| 4 | **Leader** | **Appointed by the leadership** — no threshold |

All conditions of a level must be met at the same time. "Pieces" means topics
**and** comments together.

**What each level additionally allows:**

| From level | Additional right |
|---|---|
| 1 | Writing [direct messages](/en/anleitung/produkte/private-nachrichten) |
| 3 | Renaming other people's topics and moving them to another category |
| 4 | On top of that: pinning, closing, marking as solved — and editing other people's text |

Three promises you can rely on:

- **It never goes down.** A level you reached stays, even if you write nothing
  for a while.
- **A level never takes anything away.** It only adds to what your role allows
  anyway.
- **Level 4 is granted by the owner** under **Dashboard → Trust levels** — and
  can be withdrawn the same way. The badge stays even then.

::note
You can see your own level, and what's missing to the next one, on the badges
page.
::

## Mentions

If you type an `@` in a topic or comment, Pukalani suggests matching members;
once picked, it reads `@name`. The person mentioned gets a notification.

- **The name belongs to your account.** An `@name` applies across all of
  Pukalani, not just in one community: the same person is called the same
  everywhere, and two accounts cannot hold the same name at once. Whoever
  claimed it first keeps it.
- **Nobody has to fill anything in.** Pukalani suggests a name from your
  display name; you can be mentioned from day one.
- **Changing it** works in your community's dashboard under **Settings**, or in
  the account area under **Profile** — but **at most every 30 days**.
- **Old mentions stay intact.** If you rename yourself, the old name stays
  reserved for you and keeps pointing at you in posts already written.
- **Allowed** are lower-case letters, digits and underscores, 3 to 24
  characters — an underscore may not sit at the start or the end. Some names
  are reserved (such as `pukalani`, `team`, `admin`) and cannot be chosen.
- **Mentions are highlighted but not linked** — Pukalani has no public profile
  pages.
- Only **someone who belongs to this community** can be mentioned.

## What doesn't exist today

So you don't go looking for it:

- **No separate on/off switch** for discussions. They hang off the *Posts*
  product and therefore off your plan. Once categories exist, they are there.
- **No full-text search** across post bodies and replies (see above).
- **No sub-categories** and no category colours.
- **No public profile pages**, and consequently no profile links.
- **No demotion in trust levels** and **no level by hand** except the fourth —
  the first three come solely through the thresholds.
- **No badges next to the name** in the topic list; they live in the gallery.

## Who may do what

| Action | Who |
|---|---|
| Create, change, retire categories | Admin, owner |
| Open a topic | Every signed-in member |
| Rename other people's topics, change category | Moderator and above — or trust level 3 |
| Pin, close | Moderator and above — or trust level 4 |
| Mark as solved | As above — or whoever opened the topic |
| Edit other people's text | Moderator and above — or trust level 4 |
| Grant trust level 4 | Owner only |
| Write the about text and the rules | Editor, admin, owner |

The roles in detail are under
[Members & roles](/en/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen).
