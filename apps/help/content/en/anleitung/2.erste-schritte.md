Creating a community takes a few minutes. All you need is an email address and
an invitation code.

## 1. Invitation code

Pukalani is in Early Access — new communities are created by invitation. The
code is in your invitation email and looks like `PUKA-XXXX-XXXX`. Upper and
lower case don't matter.

- The link in the email takes you straight into the setup assistant and checks
  the code without another click.
- Without a code you can leave a request: briefly describe what you want to use
  Pukalani for — your address is only used to send you access.
- The code is checked **at the start**, not at the end. Filling in seven steps
  and then being turned away would be the worst possible first experience.

::note
Checking the code does not use it up. It is only redeemed at the moment your
community actually comes into being.
::

## 2. Signing in

Signing in works with a **code sent to your email address** — you don't have to
set a password. You enter your address, receive a numeric code and type it in.
Alternatively you can sign in with a password if you have created one.

If you weren't signed in when you clicked the invitation link, you'll be taken
back to where you were headed once you have signed in.

## 3. The setup assistant

Seven steps, all of them changeable later:

::steps{level="4"}

#### Name and address

What is your community called, and at what address should it be reachable? The
address consists of lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens and produces
`yourname.pukalani.app`. Whether it is still free is checked as you type. A
working title is enough — both can be changed later.

Plus the question of why you're here: building something new, moving an
existing community, or just having a look around.

#### How many people are you bringing?

An estimate in ranges — from "nobody yet" to "more than 5,000". The answer does
not decide which features you get.

#### What are you about?

Coaching & consulting, courses & education, creators & audiences, clubs &
volunteering, companies & teams, health & sport, crafts & hobbies — or
something else.

#### Who are you bringing together?

Two sentences. They become the first version of your home page. This step can
be skipped; an AI suggestion is offered as a draft that you can read, change or
throw away.

#### What would count as real success in six months?

Building relationships, getting conversations going, collecting knowledge,
reaching more people — or events, courses and revenue. The last three are
marked *soon*: they are built, but not yet generally available.

#### Which mood fits?

Six curated starting points for colour and typeface. Later you have the full
range — 26 colour worlds with eleven shades each, plus your own fonts (see
[Design & themes](/en/anleitung/produkte/themes)).

#### Does this look right?

The summary. With that click your community comes into being — and the trial
period starts at that moment.

::

## 4. Trial period and quota

- **14 days of Pro**, with no payment details. The trial starts at the moment
  your community is created.
- After that the community becomes **read-only** until a plan is chosen: posts,
  comments and sign-ups pause. **Nothing** is deleted — content, members and
  settings are kept in full, and with a subscription everything continues
  immediately.
- **One** community during the trial, **up to three** per account afterwards.

Details on the plans: [Billing](/en/anleitung/abrechnung).

## 5. What to do straight afterwards

1. **Adjust the home page** — the draft from the assistant is a beginning, not
   a result. See [Pages](/en/anleitung/produkte/seiten).
2. **Settle the design** — colour world and fonts in Customize theme, see
   [Design & themes](/en/anleitung/produkte/themes).
3. **Write down the rules** — a page of your own with your ground rules for
   conversation makes moderation easy later, see
   [Moderation](/en/anleitung/produkte/moderation).
4. **Invite members** — and think first about who gets which role, see
   [Members & roles](/en/anleitung/mitglieder-und-rollen).

## 6. Several communities

At the top left of the dashboard sits the **community switcher**: a click on
your community's name opens the list of all communities in which you hold a
team role (owner, admin, moderator or editor). Switching signs you in on the
other side automatically — you jump from dashboard to dashboard without
logging in again.

Below the list are two exits that arrive equally signed in:

- **Create community** takes you into the setup assistant.
- **Manage communities** opens your account area at `account.pukalani.app` —
  the overview of every community you belong to.

How many communities one account can create is covered above under
[Trial period and quota](#4-trial-period-and-quota).
