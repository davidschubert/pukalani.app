Pukalani is a community platform: a place of your own where your members write,
learn and meet — under your name and your design. Unlike a group inside a large
network, this place belongs to you.

The platform is **modular**: comments, posts, pages, design and moderation can
each be switched on and off. You start lean and add what you actually need.

## Two places worth knowing

| Place | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Your community** — `yourname.pukalani.app` | Everything your members see: home page, comments, posts, pages. As the owner, you reach the administration from here via **Dashboard** — including your community's plan and billing. |
| **Your account area** — `account.pukalani.app` | The overview of every community you belong to, and the starting point for new ones. Also reachable from any dashboard through the community switcher at the top left. |

The two are separate because they belong to different things: the community
owns its content, its members and its contract — the account area belongs to
you and your account. One account can hold several communities.

## Principles you can rely on

- **Hosted in Germany.** The servers are at Hetzner in Germany.
- **No advertising trackers.** No ads, no reselling of data.
- **Sign-in that asks for little.** First-party sessions, no forced password —
  you can sign in with a code sent to your email address.
- **No one-way contract.** Export and deletion are built in; your content stays
  yours.

::note
Pukalani is currently in **Early Access**: new communities are created by
invitation. Some products are built and running but are not yet part of the
open offering — this guide marks them as *Early Access*.
::
